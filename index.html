Gaming Group

We buy studios and leave them alone.

Welcome Holdings LTD owns game studios and live-games businesses across mobile, PC and console. We hold them permanently — no fund clock, no forced merger, no renaming your studio after us.

How we acquire

A permanent home for game studios.

Welcome Holdings was founded by two studio heads who had each been acquired once and hated it. The pattern was always the same: a five-year fund clock, a publisher's roadmap dropped on the team, and the people who built the game gone within eighteen months.

So we built the opposite. We keep the studio's name, its creative director and its greenlight process. We bring the things studios lose sleep over instead — live-ops infrastructure, user acquisition budget, platform relationships, legal, and a balance sheet that can absorb a project that slips a year.

How we acquire

Three filters. If a studio does not clear all three, we pass — politely, and within a week.

01

A game that already works

Live, profitable, with players who come back. We are not a publisher and we do not fund unreleased projects — bring us a title with a retention curve, not a pitch deck.

02

A team that wants to stay

We buy studios, not founder exits. Leadership keeps a meaningful stake, keeps the creative call, and keeps the studio name over the door.

03

Something defensible

A live community, a licence, a long-running IP, or an engine advantage others would need years to copy. Download spikes are not a moat.

Our commitments to players

Every studio in the group signs up to the same rules on launch day. They are not marketing lines — they are in the acquisition agreement.

  • No pay-to-win in competitive modes — paid items may change how a game looks or how fast you progress, never whether you win.
  • Odds are published — any randomised reward shows its real drop rates in the client, before purchase.
  • Under-18 spending caps — on by default on every account we know to be a minor, in every market.
  • Servers get notice — twelve months' warning before any live game is shut down, plus an offline mode where it is technically possible.
  • Your data stays in the game — we do not sell player data to advertisers or data brokers. Ever.
